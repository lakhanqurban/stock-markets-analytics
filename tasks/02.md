2. **Valuation Discipline**

   - Compare to public comps in same sector - demand discount for illiquidity
   - Avoid IPOs priced above mid-point of original range (signals weak demand)
3. **Post-IPO Timing (Based on Q3 findings)**

   - Wait for lock-up expiration (typically 180 days) - avoids supply overhang
   - Enter after first earnings report - reduces uncertainty
   - Use 3-6 month holding period (Q3 shows median growth deteriorates over time)

5. **Sector Rotation**

   - Overweight sectors with tailwinds (AI, energy transition, GLP-1 pharma)
   - Underweight cyclical sectors near peak cycles
   - Use macro indicators from Q4 dataset (FEDFUNDS, DGS10, CPI) to time entries
