## Question 4: [EXPLORATORY] Ideal Trading Strategy

"My ideal strategy" (beyond $100-per-prediction):

1. **Signal pipeline.** Score all stocks daily with `rf_best.predict_proba` only; skip discrete 0/1 rules. Trade only when proba ≥ 0.60, sized by confidence: `weight = (proba − 0.6) / 0.4` of the per-trade budget.
2. **Trading budget & caps.** Fixed daily risk budget (e.g. $2,000). Max 1% of capital per stock-day; max 3 concurrent bets per ticker; total open exposure ≤ 6 × daily budget (30-day horizon ⇒ ~1% of book per trade).
3. **Trade frequency control.** Instead of every marginal 1, keep at most the top-5 highest-probability names per day (cuts trades/day from ~6 to ~3 ⇒ fewer $0.20$ round-trips and less chatter risk).
4. **Regime filter.** No new positions when `growth_snp500_30d < 1` and the tree's macro leaf says tightening (`DGS10 > 4.5`); occasionally one macro veto is worth more than ten stock signals.
5. **Exit rules.** Hold to 30 days unless: (a) stop at −8% from entry, (b) take profit at +15%, (c) opposite proba < 0.4 next day (model flip = early exit).
6. **Reject & validate.** Walk-forward on the TEST split only, compare CAGR/Sharpe vs always-in benchmark (invest $1,000 daily evenly, no signals). Kill the strategy if after ~0.05% slippage+fees and 1-day execution delay the net CAGR < benchmark, or if returns concentrate in <10 trading days.
